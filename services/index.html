What I build

Seven ways I make your CRM work harder

01

CRM Architecture & Setup

Your CRM, designed around how you sell.

  • Pipelines, stages and opportunity rules
  • Custom fields, tags, Smart Lists and calendars
  • Users, permissions and sub-account structure
  • Snapshots for repeatable setups
02

Workflow Automation

Follow-up that never forgets.

  • Speed-to-lead and lead nurture sequences
  • Appointment confirmations, reminders and no-show recovery
  • Payment, onboarding and membership automations
  • Internal alerts, tasks and handoffs
03

AI Agent Development

AI that works your pipeline around the clock.

  • Lead-response and qualification agents on SMS, chat and social DMs
  • Voice agents for inbound calls and booking (Vapi, Retell, Synthflow, GHL Voice AI)
  • Custom agents with OpenAI, orchestrated in n8n or Make, with tools and CRM actions
  • Knowledge bases, guardrails, human handoff and compliance defaults
04

Integrations & APIs

One source of truth across your tools.

  • Zapier, Make and n8n scenarios
  • Stripe, Twilio, Kixie and Meta Lead Ads
  • Webhooks and custom API connections
  • External site and form tracking (WordPress, Elementor)
05

CRM Migrations

Move platforms without losing a thing.

  • Salesforce, Pipedrive and HubSpot data into HighLevel
  • Systeme.io and ClickFunnels funnels rebuilt
  • Pipelines and automations copied across accounts
  • Clean-up, deduplication and verification
06

Conversation AI & Chatbots

Replies in seconds, day or night.

  • GoHighLevel Conversation AI and AI Employee
  • CloseBot setups for clinics and agencies
  • Booking logic and calendar integration
  • Prompt tuning from real conversations
07

Funnels, Sites & Portals

Front ends that feed the CRM.

  • HighLevel websites, funnels and surveys
  • Member areas and client portals
  • White-label agency dashboards and custom CSS
  • Email templates, A2P 10DLC and deliverability setup
